# Section Hierarchy

Every section must have a distinct purpose and a dominant element. If a section exists merely to "hold information," it must be redesigned or merged.

## Principles of Hierarchy

1. **Only ONE dominant element per view.** If there is a massive headline, the image must be small. If there is a massive image, the text must be minimal.
2. **Every section answers a question.**
3. **No section is equal.** We must dictate what the user cares about most.

---

## Hierarchy Map (Homepage Example)

### Section 1: The Arrival (Hero)
- **Purpose**: To hook the user and establish the visual standard.
- **Emotion**: Awe, curiosity.
- **Dominant Element**: The full-bleed photography/video.
- **Secondary Element**: The title typography.

### Section 2: The Voice (Story & Quote)
- **Purpose**: To humanize the experience immediately.
- **Emotion**: Intimacy, respect.
- **Dominant Element**: A massive pull quote (The voice of the village).
- **Secondary Element**: A small, intimate portrait or detail shot.

### Section 3: The Environment (Landscape Pause)
- **Purpose**: A visual reward; a deep breath.
- **Emotion**: Peace, vastness.
- **Dominant Element**: A full-width landscape image.
- **Secondary Element**: A tiny, elegant caption.

### Section 4: The Journey (Timeline/History)
- **Purpose**: To ground the village in time.
- **Emotion**: Nostalgia, endurance.
- **Dominant Element**: The structural line of the timeline itself.
- **Secondary Element**: Key dates in bold Swiss typography.

### Section 5: The Life (Gallery Collage)
- **Purpose**: To show daily life without forcing endless scrolling.
- **Emotion**: Energy, community.
- **Dominant Element**: The composition of the collage (the arrangement of images).
- **Secondary Element**: None. The images speak for themselves.

### Section 6: The Proof (Statistics)
- **Purpose**: To validate the village's progress and scale.
- **Emotion**: Trust, clarity.
- **Dominant Element**: Massive numerals (e.g., "1.2M", "100%").
- **Secondary Element**: Minimalist icons/labels.

### Section 7: The Invitation (Closing CTA)
- **Purpose**: To give the user a next step before they leave.
- **Emotion**: Longing.
- **Dominant Element**: The CTA button/link.
- **Secondary Element**: A warm, inviting background texture.
