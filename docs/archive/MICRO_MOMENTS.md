# Visual Film Language: Micro-Moments

A micro-moment is an unexpected reward delivered during the scroll. It occurs every 5 to 10 seconds of interaction. It requires zero cognitive effort to consume.

## The Catalog of Rewards

### 1. The Isolated Sentence
- **Execution:** The user scrolls past a dense block of text and images. The next viewport contains only the background color and one line of text: "They never lock their doors." 
- **Reward:** Instant emotional context without reading effort.

### 2. The Tiny Motion
- **Execution:** A static landscape image occupies the screen. In the corner of the image, a 40px by 40px square video loops continuously, showing only a hand weaving a single thread of bamboo.
- **Reward:** Discovery of a hidden, living detail within a static environment.

### 3. The Unanchored Caption
- **Execution:** A large photograph of an empty wooden chair sits on the left. The right side is blank. The user scrolls. 200 pixels below the chair, floating in empty space, is a 12px italic caption: "Where the village head sat for 40 years."
- **Reward:** The delayed context creates a sudden shift in how the preceding image is perceived.

### 4. The Scale Shift
- **Execution:** The user scrolls through three consecutive 200px wide square photos. The fourth scroll reveals an image that stretches 120vw, forcing horizontal overflow, showing a massive river.
- **Reward:** The sudden breaking of the established grid creates physical surprise.

### 5. The Audio Implication
- **Execution:** An image of a laughing child. Overlaid across the bottom edge of the image in 8rem text is the word: "Hahahaha." The text is clipped by the image container.
- **Reward:** The brain "hears" the sound because the typography mimics the suddenness of laughter.

## Rule of Frequency
If the user scrolls for 15 seconds and encounters only standard paragraphs and centered images, they are bored. We must inject a micro-moment to break the trance.
