# Page-by-Page Rewrite Strategy

## 1. Homepage
- **Current Layout**: Hero -> Story -> Nature -> Potential -> Timeline -> Gallery -> Transparency -> CTA
- **Problems**: Exhaustingly long. Every section demands equal attention. Too many large images waste scroll real estate. The dark backgrounds feel like a tech startup, not a village.
- **Rewrite Proposal**:
  - **Hero (Full Bleed)**: Total immersion. A strong natural image (e.g., river at sunrise), no distracting UI. 
  - **Story + Quote (Gabung)**: Merge the history paragraph with a dominant quote. Use an asymmetrical layout (large text left, small image right).
  - **Landscape**: A massive, full-width cinematic shot of the geography. Pure emotion, no text.
  - **Timeline (Compact)**: Shift from long vertical scrolling to a tight, horizontal or grid-based layout.
  - **Gallery Collage**: Combine 5-6 images into a single, beautifully composed collage block.
  - **Statistics**: Massive typography (e.g., "1.2M", "24/7", "100%") with minimalist icons.
  - **Closing**: A warm quote, a beautiful image, and the final CTA.
- **Impact**: Reduced scroll depth by 40%. The user thinks: *"Wait, why is a village website this beautiful?"*

## 2. Profile (Desa)
- **Current Layout**: Predictable blocks of text and standard portrait images.
- **Problems**: Feels like reading a Wikipedia page or a corporate "About Us".
- **Rewrite Proposal**: Treat it like a feature interview in Patagonia Stories. 
  - Introduce the village through the eyes of its oldest resident.
  - Use massive pull quotes to break up the narrative. 
  - Use asymmetrical image placements on a warm paper background.

## 3. Geography (Alam)
- **Current Layout**: Map -> Stats -> Description.
- **Problems**: Dry, informational, lacks the feeling of the environment.
- **Rewrite Proposal**: "The Lay of the Land". 
  - Start with a cinematic wide shot that feels like morning mist.
  - Integrate geographical statistics directly onto landscape images as subtle overlays or adjacent, clean captions.

## 4. Governance (Transparansi & Pemerintahan)
- **Current Layout**: Org chart, lists of officials, transparency numbers.
- **Problems**: Bureaucratic, boring, looks like a dashboard.
- **Rewrite Proposal**: "The Guardians". 
  - Focus on the faces and the philosophy of the leaders. 
  - High-contrast portraiture (warm lighting) with short, powerful quotes. 
  - Transparency numbers presented as elegant, editorial infographics.

## 5. Potentials (Potensi)
- **Current Layout**: Grid of cards.
- **Problems**: Looks like an e-commerce catalog or a SaaS feature list.
- **Rewrite Proposal**: A photo-essay approach. 
  - Staggered imagery. 
  - Let the visuals of the village's potential (agriculture, culture) speak louder than descriptive text.

## 6. Information (Berita/Agenda)
- **Current Layout**: Standard list/feed.
- **Problems**: Lacks hierarchy, feels like a standard blog.
- **Rewrite Proposal**: Editorial bulletin. 
  - Clean, Swiss-inspired typographic layout. 
  - Extreme whitespace. 
  - Let the headlines dominate.
