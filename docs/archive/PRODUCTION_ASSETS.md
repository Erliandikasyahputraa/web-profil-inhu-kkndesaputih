# Production Assets Inventory

## Current Status (RC2)
The project currently relies on high-quality placeholder images sourced from Unsplash. These must be replaced with official client assets prior to final release.

## Required Assets
To maintain the visual integrity of the Digital Coffee Table Book, the client must provide:

### Homepage
- 1x Hero Video or sweeping landscape image (21:9 aspect ratio recommended).
- 4x Event/Community images (square or portrait).

### Profile
- 1x Historic/Heritage image (portrait or landscape).
- 2x Vision/Mission images (abstract or civic-focused).

### Geography
- 1x Map graphic or wide landscape.
- 4x Infrastructure/Border photos.

### Governance
- 1x Village Head portrait (high-resolution, muted background preferred).
- 1x Balai Desa exterior/interior shot.
- Staff headshots (optional, currently using list format).

### Potentials
- 5x Industry feature photos (Agriculture, Plantation, MSME, Fishery, Tourism).
- 1x Gallery grid (2-6 supplementary photos).

### Information
- 1x Civic service desk or community gathering photo.
- 1x Document/Archive abstract photo.

## Technical Requirements
- **Format:** WebP or optimized JPEG.
- **Resolution:** Min 2000px width for hero images. Min 1080px for portraits.
- **Alt Text:** Descriptive alt text must be provided for every final image.
