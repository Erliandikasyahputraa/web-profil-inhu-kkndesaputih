# The Visitor Journey Map

This map traces the emotional arc of the visitor from the second they arrive until the second they leave. It dictates how they should feel at every stage of their descent into the documentary.

## 1. The Threshold (0-10% of the Journey)
- **The State:** Skeptical, rushed, distracted by the modern world.
- **Our Goal:** Arrest their momentum. Force a deep breath.
- **The Feeling:** Awe and sudden quiet. They should feel like they just stepped off a loud street into a silent, sunlit forest. 

## 2. The Hook (10-25% of the Journey)
- **The State:** Intrigued, their scanning slows down.
- **Our Goal:** Make them lean closer. Introduce the human element.
- **The Feeling:** Intimacy. A soft smile. A feeling of recognition when they read a profound quote that resonates with their own humanity.

## 3. The Immersion (25-50% of the Journey)
- **The State:** Engaged, actively exploring.
- **Our Goal:** Provide visual richness and rhythm without exhausting them.
- **The Feeling:** Discovery. The joy of turning over a stone to see what's underneath. They feel the energy and life of the community through dense, art-directed collages.

## 4. The Cinematic Pause (50-60% of the Journey)
- **The State:** Absorbing information rapidly, slightly stimulated.
- **Our Goal:** Cleanse the palate. Stop the flow of information.
- **The Feeling:** Profound peace. Nostalgia for a place they have never been. A moment of silence where they just look at a beautiful landscape and think.

## 5. The Validation (60-85% of the Journey)
- **The State:** Emotionally invested, looking for grounding facts.
- **Our Goal:** Prove that the emotion is backed by reality. Provide the statistics and scale.
- **The Feeling:** Clarity and respect. "This place isn't just beautiful; it is thriving and real."

## 6. The Call (85-100% of the Journey)
- **The State:** Satisfied but longing.
- **Our Goal:** Provide the bridge to the real world.
- **The Feeling:** A quiet resolve. The decision has been made in their heart. They look at the final invitation and think, "I need to go there."
