# RC4: The Interactive Editorial Documentary
**Identity**: Immersive Documentary Experience
**Benchmark**: Patagonia Stories (Philosophy, Storytelling, Rhythm)
**Mood**: Morning Light, Nostalgia, Nature, Peace

## The Paradigm Shift: From Website to Documentary
We are officially abandoning the term "Coffee Table Book." That concept misled the design towards giant photos, endless whitespace, and excessively long scrolling. 

This is an **Interactive Editorial Documentary**. 

A documentary relies on rhythm, narrative, tension, and emotion. It is not a collection of beautiful, static pages. It is a journey. 

Every design decision must answer: *"Would Patagonia Stories tell the story this way?"*

### 1. Destroying the "Frontend" Mindset
We are no longer thinking in terms of:
- Sections
- Components
- Cards
- Containers
- Grids
- Rows
- Columns
- Buttons

We are thinking in terms of **Emotional Beats**:
- **Arrival** (The Hook)
- **Silence** (The Pause)
- **Curiosity** (The Question)
- **Discovery** (The Unveiling)
- **Memory** (The History)
- **Emotion** (The Human Element)
- **Pause** (The Cinematic Breath)
- **Reflection** (The Meaning)
- **Departure** (The Legacy)

Each scroll should represent one emotional beat, not one React component.

### 2. Aesthetic Identity (Warmth & Memory)
We are eliminating dark themes entirely, except where emotionally required (e.g., a literal night scene). Dark backgrounds destroy the identity of a morning village, sunlight, mist, and nature. 
- **The Palette**: Warm paper, soft stone, cream, light olive, muted beige, sage, terracotta, warm white.
- **The Lighting**: Morning sunlight, soft afternoon light.
- **The Texture**: Natural linen, wood, soil, grass.

### 3. Content Compression (The Patagonia Rhythm)
Paragraphs must be short. Images must be earned.
- We will not use giant images just to fill space. 
- We will not use long paragraphs. We will use powerful sentences, dominant quotes, and dense collages.
- The user must never feel tired. Scrolling should feel like discovery, not labor.
