# Documentary Rules

## Emotional Rules
1. Curiosity before Information.
2. Emotion before Statistics.
3. Show before Explain.
4. Every scroll must earn the next scroll.
5. People scan before they read.
6. The visitor should feel something before learning something.

## Narrative Rules (The Chapters)
1. **Arrival**: Establish the mood. Is this place worth caring about?
2. **The People**: Intimate, human connection. Who breathes life into this place?
3. **The Land**: The physical reality. What shapes these people?
4. **The Guardians**: Stewardship and philosophy. Who protects this peace?
5. **The Livelihood**: Dignity of labor. How does this community sustain its future?
6. **The Connection**: The bridge to reality. How do I step into this story?

## Curiosity Rules
- Use the incomplete hook (show a detail, make them ask "what is this?").
- Use provocative statements instead of generic summaries.
- Contrast dense visuals with sudden emptiness.
- Provide a breadcrumb trail to the next chapter.

## Eye Flow & Attention
- **First Fixation**: The anchor (massive typography or imagery).
- **Second Fixation**: The context.
- **Third Fixation**: The detail/curiosity point.
- **Exit Point**: The scroll trigger.
- **Rule**: Nothing should compete. Reduce elements until the sequence is clear.

## Visual Density
- **Inhale**: Extreme emptiness (e.g., 95% whitespace, one sentence).
- **Hold Breath**: High density (e.g., tight collages, overlapping elements).
- **Exhale**: Structured emptiness (e.g., one massive statistic and blank space).
- **Deep Breath**: The void (e.g., full-bleed landscape, no UI).
- **Rule**: Never repeat the same density block consecutively.

## Cinematography
- **Wide Shot**: Establishing the space. Full bleed.
- **Medium Shot**: Context and action.
- **Portrait**: Human anchor. Eye contact.
- **Extreme Close Up**: Texture, hands, details.
- **Landscape**: Horizontal dominance.
- **Rule**: Alternate framing constantly to change psychological distance.

## Micro Moments
- One memorable moment every 5–10 seconds of scrolling.
- Examples: An isolated sentence, a tiny motion loop, an unanchored caption, a sudden scale shift.

## Visitor Journey
- **The Threshold**: Awe and sudden quiet.
- **The Hook**: Intimacy and a smile.
- **The Immersion**: Discovery and energy.
- **The Cinematic Pause**: Profound peace and nostalgia.
- **The Validation**: Clarity and respect (statistics).
- **The Call**: Quiet resolve and longing to visit.
