# Project Bible

## Project Vision
An Immersive Editorial Documentary. This is not a village website; it is an interactive experience designed to make the visitor feel the morning mist, the warmth of the community, and the peace of nature.

## Design Philosophy
Every scroll must earn the next scroll. We prioritize emotion over information, curiosity over exposition, and narrative rhythm over generic frontend layouts.

## Visual Identity
- **Mood**: Scandinavian Minimalism (Emotion) meets Swiss Design (Layout).
- **Core Elements**: Morning light, warm paper, nature, wood, mist, river, rice fields, nostalgia, warmth, peace.
- **Strict Constraint**: Dark backgrounds are forbidden unless explicitly required by the emotional narrative (e.g., a literal night scene).

## Color Direction
- **Primary**: Warm paper, cream, soft stone.
- **Accents**: Light olive, muted beige, sage, terracotta.
- **Typography**: Deep forest green, warm brown, or soft charcoal (never pure black or stark white).

## Typography Direction
- **Display**: Massive serif or elegant sans-serif (e.g., 8rem+). Used exclusively for critical hooks and dominant quotes.
- **Detail**: Tiny (12px) captions and labels. 
- **Body**: Minimized. We avoid large blocks of text. Reading must never feel like a chore.
- **Contrast**: Extreme contrast between Display and Detail. No "muddy middle ground".

## References & Inspiration
- **Primary Reference**: Patagonia Stories.
- **Secondary Reference**: NYT Snow Fall.
- **The Question**: "Would Patagonia Stories present this information this way?" If no, redesign it.
